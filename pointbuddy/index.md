# Keep score in your favorite tabletop games

PointBuddy tracks points, turns and timers around the table, so you can focus on playing.

- **Score & turn tracking** with a full history and undo, custom rules and presets.
- **Compass mode:** point your phone at a player to give them the turn.
- **Rankings & Elo ratings** across your games, by ruleset or group of players.
- **PointBuddy Pro:** hands-free voice scoring and curated presets for popular games.

Coming soon to Google Play.
