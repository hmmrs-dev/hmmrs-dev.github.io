# Your companion at the game table

Whether it's a quick game over lunch with colleagues or a long evening with friends, Turno keeps
track of points, turns and time, so everyone can focus on playing.

- **Score pad:** add points with a tap, or any amount. Every entry is written down in the history,
  and can be undone when someone miscounts.
- **Turns & timers:** point your phone at a player to give them the turn, with an optional turn
  timer or stopwatch.
- **Your usual table:** saved players keep their color, and groups set up the table in one tap.
- **Rankings & Elo ratings** across your games, by ruleset or group of players.
- **Turno Pro:** hands-free voice scoring and curated presets for popular games.
