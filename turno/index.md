# Your companion at the game table

Turno is an Android score keeper for tabletop games. Whether it's a quick game over lunch with
colleagues or a long evening with friends, it keeps track of points, turns and time, so everyone can
focus on playing.

- **Score pad:** add points with a tap, or any amount. Every entry is written down in the history,
  and can be undone when someone miscounts.
- **Turns & timers:** point your phone at a player to give them the turn, with an optional turn
  timer or stopwatch.
- **Your usual table:** saved players keep their color, and groups set up the table in one tap.
- **Rankings & Elo ratings** across your games, by ruleset or group of players.
- **Turno Pro:** hands-free voice scoring, sync folders to share your games, and curated presets
  for popular games.

Turno is made by {{ site.developer }}, from an original idea by Diego Romani.

Coming soon to Google Play.

## Sync folders and Google Drive

Sync folders, part of Turno Pro, let the people you play with get each other's games: Turno saves
your finished games in a folder you share, and adds the games the others saved there. A sync folder
can be on your device, or in your Google Drive.

If you pick a folder in Google Drive, Turno asks for access to your Drive through Google's consent
screen. The games in a shared folder are saved there by the other players' copies of Turno, and
Google only lets an app read files like these with access to your whole Drive: that's why the
consent screen asks for it. Turno uses this access only for sync folders:

- it lists the folders of your Drive and the ones shared with you, for you to pick one, and creates
  a folder when you ask it to;
- it reads and writes game files only in the folders you picked;
- it reads the email address of your Google account, to use the right account for each folder.

It doesn't open, change or delete any other file in your Drive. Turno talks to Google Drive directly
from your device: nothing goes through me, and I have no access to your Drive. You can remove
Turno's access at any time from your [Google Account](https://myaccount.google.com/connections).
