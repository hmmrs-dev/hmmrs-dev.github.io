# hmrs-tech.github.io

Public pages of hmrs.tech apps, published with GitHub Pages at https://hmrs-tech.github.io/.

Each app has its own folder:

| App | Page | English | Italian |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| PointBuddy | Home | [`pointbuddy/index.md`](pointbuddy/index.md) | [`pointbuddy/it/index.md`](pointbuddy/it/index.md) |
| PointBuddy | Privacy policy | [`pointbuddy/privacy/index.md`](pointbuddy/privacy/index.md) | [`pointbuddy/it/privacy/index.md`](pointbuddy/it/privacy/index.md) |

The PointBuddy app opens its privacy policy in its current language, so the URLs of the two privacy
pages (`/pointbuddy/privacy/` and `/pointbuddy/it/privacy/`) must not change. When a policy changes,
update both languages and the "last updated" date at the top of each.

The contact email and the developer name are set once, in [`_config.yml`](_config.yml), which also
sets the name and icons shared by all the pages of each app.
